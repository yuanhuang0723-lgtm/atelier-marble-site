# Atelier Marble SEO Keyword Map

Updated: 2026-09-08

## Strategy

The site targets English-language international buyers looking for a stone supplier, fabrication partner, or project quotation from China. Each indexable page gets one primary search intent. Related terms are placed in the page copy and internal links naturally; no hidden keyword blocks or keyword-stuffed homepage sections are used.

Search-volume and difficulty numbers are intentionally not fabricated. The available generic Bing result pages were noisy and did not provide reliable supplier SERP evidence. Replace these hypotheses with Google Search Console queries and Keyword Planner data after the new domain has accumulated data.

Google autocomplete supplied a small set of usable language signals on 2026-09-08, including `natural stone supplier China`, `architectural stone suppliers`, `architectural stone manufacturers`, and `bathroom vanity top manufacturers`. These are wording signals, not volume or ranking evidence. `sintered stone supplier China` was observed but is not assigned to a page because the current site does not establish that product scope.

## On-page rules for future work

- Keep one clear H1 per page and put the primary phrase in the title, H1, opening copy, URL, and description where it reads naturally.
- Keep the homepage focused on brand, applications, proof, and one clear quote route. Detailed workflow belongs on `/how-we-work`; detailed project evidence belongs on project pages.
- Use short paragraphs, restrained cards, and contextual internal links. Do not create a new keyword card for every phrase.
- Keep claims tied to visible factory/project evidence. Do not add unsupported production capacity, certifications, client names, ratings, or delivery promises.
- Review title length after the shared `| Atelier Marble` template; do not optimize raw child titles alone.
- Use Google Search Console impressions, queries, CTR, and conversions to choose the next content update. Ranking is not proven by a green build or by a page being present in the sitemap.
