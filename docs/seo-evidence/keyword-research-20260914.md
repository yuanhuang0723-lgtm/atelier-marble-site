# Keyword Research Evidence

- Date: 2026-09-14
- Market: global English-language B2B stone buyers
- Queries reviewed: custom stone fabrication manufacturer; cut to size stone fabrication; custom stone countertops; stone fabrication China
- Purpose: content gap research, not ranking or search-volume measurement

## Repeated buyer information

The reviewed results commonly explain the application, material, dimensions, quantity, drawings or reference images, cut-outs and edges, inspection, packing, and destination. They use a quote or drawing-submission action close to the relevant scope.

## Evidence patterns

- JSC Stone presents manufacturing, fabrication, wholesale supply, slabs, countertops, vanity tops, and custom products for global projects.
- HD Stone emphasizes cut-to-size work, custom fabrication, materials, export, and factory capability.
- George Group exposes a customization matrix covering stone type, application, dimensions, thickness, finishes, edges, packaging, and a staged export example.
- Apexstone asks for application, stone or reference image, dimensions, quantity, destination, and required date before fabrication review.
- Natural Stone Veneer and The Stone Collection make cut-to-size scope and inspection or dispatch requirements visible.

## Decisions for Atelier Marble

- Keep custom fabrication and cut-to-size work on one primary page until GSC proves separate intent.
- Keep custom countertops on a dedicated page with application-specific fields.
- Use manufacturer or factory wording only where Atelier Marble can substantiate the business role and capability.
- Do not copy unsupported claims about owned factories, machinery, capacity, certifications, installation, years, or country coverage.
- Keep the quote path close to dimensions, drawings, quantities, material, inspection, and packing content.

## Limitations

Search result snippets are not search volume, stable ranking evidence, or proof of competitor claims. Confirm future keyword decisions with original Search Console query and page data.
